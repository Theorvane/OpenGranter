## Direct Anthropic capacity mapping (#534)

Both client bases now accept normalized `service_tier:"auto"|"default"` on registered direct Anthropic requests across nonstream/text/refusal/function streaming. Map `auto` to native `auto`; map explicit `default` to native `standard_only`, restricting that request to standard capacity. Omission/null still omits the upstream field, with no inserted tier or change to native defaults. This supersedes the earlier blanket Anthropic supplied-tier rejection only. The native literal `standard_only` remains outside the public normalized enum; other recognized Anthropic tiers still reject before credentials. Managed OpenAI, delegated OpenRouter and Gemini retain their existing subsets and endpoint-scope constraints.

The shared direct transport validates the captured primitive before credentials and the common native body builder performs the two-value mapping. Text/function stream invokers reuse it. Preserve the registered Anthropic host, approved upstream model, output cap and2023-06-01 header; do not add beta headers, native speed mode, region selection or new provider authority. Default/auto admission changes no catalog eligibility or final-provider IAM. Existing approved destination evaluation, limits, mandatory audit/usage before final delivery, missing usage, possible billing, failures and body cancellation remain shared.

Request preferences do not prove the actual served tier, priority eligibility, latency, SLA, price or available capacity. Do not synthesize client `service_tier` or operational metadata from the requested value. Native served-tier response projection remains separate follow-up work; this request mapping does not add it. Tier fields/native strings, private text and credentials remain outside operational events/errors and synthetic response echoes.
