# Delegated final stream token usage details

Both chat bases preserve the bounded [nonstream token categories](nonstream-token-details.md) from the actual final delegated OpenRouter usage event. Both terminal-choice and empty-choice upstream usage shapes are supported. Null/empty/omitted groups, zero counts and nullable completion counters stay exact; malformed groups are omitted independently and unknown keys/cost/server-tool payloads are excluded. Missing groups are unavailable information, never zero or complete detailed reporting.

The decoder normalizes aggregate counters with its existing total-derivation rule. Independent sequence/outcome/encoder snapshots capture counters and nested groups once without inventing missing totals. Snapshots are immutable and allowlisted; earlier deltas cannot supply final details or metadata. Categories may overlap/exceed aggregates and never add to ledger totals, costs or attempt count. Content/refusal/reasoning/details stay out of final usage content.

Only complete valid aggregate usage produces a final usage frame. Partial/invalid/missing usage remains explicit in the ledger and produces no fabricated usage frame; detail-only usage remains missing. Final usage/DONE requires successful required ledger and outcome audit persistence. Authentication, model/final-provider IAM/Deny, limits, cancellation/backpressure and interruption audit remain unchanged. Unknown payloads, credentials and response bodies stay out of operational records/errors; token categories remain client-only, not new ledger metadata.

Actual pinned OpenRouter/OpenAI SDK socket cases verify the supported subset. Native/direct/tool streaming, category ledger reporting, cost/BYOK/server-tool projection and complete external-client certification remain open. The version-15 shared ChatUsage pin already tracks the inline category structure; no source refresh is required. Source: [official schema](https://openrouter.ai/openapi.json).
